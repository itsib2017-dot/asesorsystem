## 1. Business context

Lembaga Sertifikasi Profesi (LSP) certify workers against national competency standards (SKKNI) under BNSP. The assessor (*asesor kompetensi*) is the person who collects evidence, judges it against the standard, and recommends Kompeten (K) or Belum Kompeten (BK) per candidate. The LSP's technical committee / pleno makes the certification decision; BNSP issues the certificate.

The business problem is not a lack of forms — BNSP's MUK 2023 defines the forms precisely (FR.APL, FR.MAPA, FR.IA, FR.AK). The problem is the **manual, unrecorded work between the forms**: messy Drive folders, spreadsheet completeness tracking, eyeballing 20+ DIT papers and written tests for copying, matching names across three documents by eye, and assembling FR.AK.05 by hand. Each of those steps is slow, error-prone, and unauditable — and each error lands on the certificate, the assessment record, or a surveillance audit.

AsesorKompetensi exists to make that middle work fast, consistent, and auditable, while keeping every judgement with the assessor.

## 2. Business objectives

| # | Objective | Business value |
|---|---|---|
| BO-1 | Reduce assessor effort per batch by ≥ 70% on intake, completeness, and first-pass grading | An assessor can handle a larger batch, or the same batch with more attention on the presentation and interview — the parts only a human can judge |
| BO-2 | Eliminate certificate-name errors caused by mismatched source documents | A wrong name on a BNSP certificate breaks verification and requires reissue; the AI name jury (local KTP OCR + verdict, KTP-authoritative per D6) makes the error visible before AK.05 is finalised, and the assessor confirms every name |
| BO-3 | Make every assessment decision replayable for a BNSP surveillance audit | Audit evidence (inputs, prompt + model version, AI output, jury verdict, signature application, human confirmation) is stored, not reconstructed from memory |
| BO-4 | Detect copy-paste between candidates systematically instead of by chance | Protects the VATM "Asli" (authenticity) rule and the credibility of the LSP's certificates |
| BO-5 | Reuse the same system for any scheme, not only Ahli K3 Umum | The LSP's investment is not stranded on one scheme; onboarding a new scheme is config, not a rebuild — each scheme registers its own manifest (file set, question counts, rules, prerequisites, requirements; D17, v1.4) |
| BO-6 | Keep the cost of a live batch within ≈ $30–50 per batch-month | The tool must be affordable for an LSP/TUK running sporadic batches; a tool that costs more than the assessment it supports will not be used. LLM cost depends on the configured provider/model (D18) — free tiers may be $0 |
| BO-7 | Every generated document that requires an assessor signature carries that assessor's own signature, applied at sign-off and recorded | No more print-sign-rescan loops and no unsigned packs at handoff; the signature record (whose, which version, when) is itself audit evidence |

## 5. Business rules (normative)

1. **Decision values.** The only certification-recommendation values are **K (Kompeten)** and **BK (Belum Kompeten)**. "Kompeten Bersyarat" does not exist — not a decision value, a status, or a recommendation. It does not appear in the AK3U scheme document (§9.3.4) or the 04B form, which only offer Kompeten / Belum Kompeten (D15, v1.4; OQ-03 closed).
2. **AK.05 is per assessor per batch.** Where several assessors share a batch, each submits their own AK.05; the Ketua Tim consolidates. The system must not merge assessors' AK.05 reports into one unsigned document.
3. **AK.05 is a recommendation, not a decision.** The pleno decides certification. The system must model and display the handoff stages separately.
4. **VATM on every piece of evidence.** Evidence is judged Valid, Asli (authentic), Terkini (current), Memadai (sufficient). A similarity flag speaks to "Asli" and is judged by the assessor; it never auto-fails.
5. **Professional judgement on essays.** The FR.IA.06 form itself requires the assessor to use professional judgement on whether an answer is acceptable. AI grading is a recommendation; the assessor's judgement is the decision.
6. **Certificate name is AI-jury adjudicated, human-confirmed; the KTP is the authority (D6, decided v1.3).** Local KTP OCR extracts the name; the AI jury compares APL.01 vs surat tugas vs ijazah against the KTP (OCR text) and issues a verdict (match / mismatch-resolved / needs-human) with reasoning and evidence spans. **The certificate name is the KTP name verbatim, word for word**; a mismatch-resolved verdict always proposes the KTP spelling — the jury never invents or picks a spelling from another source, and it expands abbreviations using the KTP only. Worked example: surat tugas says "Declan Rice P." but the KTP says "Declan Rice Pangabean" → the final certificate name is "Declan Rice Pangabean". The verdict becomes the certificate name only after the assessor confirms it (one tap) or overrides it. The system never auto-merges or auto-corrects a name, and no jury verdict ever silently becomes final. Needs-human when the KTP itself is unclear or OCR confidence is low.
7. **DIT = Daftar Instruksi Terstruktur.** The system uses this name everywhere.
8. **Official 04B ≠ 13-question bank.** The official FR.IA.04B is the presentation assessment — verified (D14, v1.4): 4 Kelompok Pekerjaan, 13 built-in questions (one per unit; count is the scheme's manifest value), Ya/Tidak per KUK, binary recommendation; its evidence is the DIT task + presentation. The user's 13-question bank is a separate artefact with a per-scheme count (13 is AK3U's value).
9. **No hard-coded scheme data (D17/D21, v1.4).** Units, file manifests, question counts, suffix mappings, assessment/decision rules, prerequisites, registration requirements, thresholds, and retention periods are config. The AK3U 13-unit list (Kepmenaker 38/2019) is the default seed for AK3U; the AK3U values (13 questions, SLTA+3yr / D3-D4-S1+2yr / S2-S3+1yr, the AK3U evidence list) are AK3U's manifest values only — e.g. Petugas K3 has a different file set, and its values are unknown and never invented.
10. **BNSP certificate ≠ Kemnaker appointment.** The system must not imply in any wording that a BNSP Ahli K3 Umum certificate replaces the Kemnaker SK Penunjukan Ahli K3 (a separate Kemnaker process).
11. **Export rule (D8, decided).** AK.05 is exported as PDF + DOCX. Every other generated document is DOCX only. In-app review records stay in-app. Tabular data extracts (audit-log extract, completeness matrix) may additionally be XLSX as data, not documents.
12. **Assessor signature (D11).** The assessor uploads a signature once; it is stored versioned in private Storage and applied at sign-off/finalise only — never on drafts — to documents whose blank carries an assessor signature block (AK.05, AK.02, AK.01, AK.06, AK.07, 04A, 04B, 07, 08, 09, 10, APL.01, APL.02, Berita Acara). Each application records signature version + timestamp. AK.05 submit is blocked while the assessor has no active signature. A signature is never applied to another assessor's documents, and never where the blank has no assessor block (AK.03 (no signature block in the blank), AK.04 (asesi signature only), MAPA.02 (no signature block), VA (no signature block); MAPA.01's relevant-person confirmation table (incl. a Lead Asesor row) is UNCONFIRMED (OQ-17)). Assessee signature blocks are out of scope for generation.
13. **Written-test judging shape (D7, v1.3) + roll-up (D16, v1.4).** Per-question verdicts Benar / Sebagian / Salah, each with a Bahasa Indonesia explanation, plus a per-student error summary ("Ringkasan Kesalahan" — which question numbers were wrong per student). The plagiarism analysis is a separate qualitative report (status Bersih / Waspada + reasoning), never a numeric plagiarism verdict. No overall percentage is shown as the decision. **Roll-up (D16):** the AI judges VATM sufficiency of the evidence per unit and recommends K/BK; the assessor confirms or overrides. The rule is the AK3U scheme document §9.3.3–9.3.4 — evidence meeting VATM → Kompeten, otherwise Belum Kompeten — with no numeric pass mark anywhere in the scheme.
14. **Upload flow (D13, v1.4).** A scheme and its scheme-level forms (06A, 06B) are registered once per scheme. Per batch the assessor supplies only 1–2 Drive links + the Kelompok Asesi (Drive link or .xlsx). Filled per-candidate copies of 04A/04B/09 are ingested from the Drive folder; direct file upload is only a fallback. Blank forms (registered once) are distinct from filled copies (ingested per batch).
15. **Provider-agnostic, free-first, BYOK (D18, v1.4).** The user brings their own key and picks the provider (Anthropic, OpenAI-compatible, Gemini, OpenRouter / OmniRouter / 9Router, Nvidia NIM, etc.); provider + base URL/gateway + model + key are runtime config; keys are stored locally (browser) and sent per request, never logged, never committed; free tiers are usable. Grading/jury verdicts stay at temperature 0; temperature is a per-prompt config value.
16. **Similarity thresholds are numeric, with details (D19, v1.4).** Similarity flags use per-scope numeric thresholds with evidence details (spans, shared unique errors). Threshold values are UNCONFIRMED (calibration or Moza's call) — no number invented. The qualitative Bersih/Waspada report stays as the human-facing summary alongside the numbers.
17. **Zero hardcoding (D21, v1.4 — governing rule).** NO HARDCODED VALUES AT ALL, FOR ANYTHING — the system is config-based throughout. Scheme names and file manifests, question counts (never a literal 13 in code — 13 is only the AK3U manifest's value), decision/assessment rules, thresholds, prerequisites, requirements, roles, export formats, providers, models, temperatures, UI labels — all config. A fallback for a missing config is itself a config value, not a code constant.

## 7. Scope

**In scope (v1):** BR-01–BR-10, BR-13, BR-15–BR-27; PRD features F-01–F-12, F-27–F-36; deliverables D1–D19.

**In scope (intermediate):** BR-11, BR-12.

**Out of scope (v1):** candidate portal; payment; scheduling; certificate printing; BNSP submission; SSO; multi-LSP tenancy; on-premise deployment; cloud OCR as the default path; any auto-decision without assessor confirmation.

**Assumptions:**
- A1. The LSP provides its scheme document, blank FR forms, and the AK.05 layout before build Phase 4 (export validation). **Satisfied for AK3U in v1.1 (blanks) and v1.4 (scheme document):** real blank forms are extracted from the repo (`research/form-blanks-extracted.md`); the scheme document itself is verified (`research/skema-ak3u-2021-extracted.md` — SKEMA SERTIFIKASI OKUPASI AHLI K3 UMUM 2021, LSP HSE INDONESIA; no numbered KKNI level; 13 units; VATM decision rule, no numeric pass mark). Currency of the 2021 scheme vs the 2023 MUK is still one LSP check (OQ-11).
- A2. Drive folders and Sheets shared with the assessor are public ("anyone with the link") — API-key ingest applies. Non-public folders are a later phase (service-account/OAuth).
- A3. Assessors hold a valid BNSP No. Reg (MET) and the LSP issues the surat tugas.
- A4. The batch used for the pilot is the real 12 Nov 2026 AK3U batch (23 candidates) or an equivalent real batch, not synthetic data alone.
- A5. The LSP accepts an uploaded signature image (scan/photo/drawn) on generated documents as the assessor's signature — **UNCONFIRMED** (OQ-17). If it does not, generated documents must be printed and hand-signed, and the signature feature becomes a draft-only aid.

**Retention — no single verified rule.** Sources conflict (3 years for remote-assessment video per a secondary source; cert-validity + 5 years for records per one LSP's privacy policy; 4 years for video/audio per the same policy; 3.5 years per a vendor). **Encrypted-06B handling — cost/benefit note (v1.1).** Cost: no vendor cost. The cost is assessor time — one password entry per grading session, plus a per-batch confirmation step if the FR.IA.07 key is used as a fallback. Benefit: grading runs against the real 06B key instead of a reconstructed or guessed key, which is the only defensible basis for per-item verdicts and for the AK.02 record. The alternative (rebuilding the key from the 07 blank without confirmation) costs nothing in time and risks grading against the wrong key — rejected (BR-20). **v1.3 (D12):** the working AK3U key is verified as a plain docx with 13 prose answers — decomposing acceptable points from prose is part of grading setup (assessor-confirmed), still no vendor cost; the password holder is the assessor at runtime.

**AI name jury + assessor signature — cost/benefit note (v1.2).** Cost: no vendor cost. KTP OCR is local Tesseract (already in the stack for document extraction); the jury is one LLM call per candidate at the configured provider's rates (D18; within the earlier ≈ $0.01–0.03 per candidate GPT-4o-mini estimate); signature storage is a few kilobytes per assessor in the existing private bucket. The cost is assessor attention — one confirm/override tap per certificate name, and one signature upload at onboarding. Benefit: the jury replaces eyeballing four name sources per candidate (the highest-impact error in the batch — a wrong certificate name), and uploaded signatures remove the print-sign-rescan loop from every signed document.
