## The decision rule — VATM, no number (§9.3) — grounds OQ-04

- §9.3.1 Uji kompetensi methods: **observasi langsung / praktik demonstrasi, pertanyaan
  tertulis, pertanyaan lisan, verifikasi portofolio, wawancara** and other reliable, objective
  methods, consistent with the scheme.
- §9.3.3 Evidence collected in the competency test is evaluated to ensure it reflects the
  required evidence to show competence has met the **VATM** evidence rules.
- §9.3.4 Test results that have met the VATM evidence rules are recommended
  **"Kompeten"**; those that have not are recommended **"Belum Kompeten"**.
  There is NO numeric pass mark, percentage, or score cut anywhere in the scheme.
  This closes the shape of OQ-04: criterion-based (VATM sufficiency per unit), decided by
  the AI's judgement of the evidence and confirmed by the assessor — Moza's "AI judge it
  for me" (2026-11-18). "Kompeten Bersyarat" does not appear anywhere: OQ-03 closed.
- §9.3.5 The assessor delivers the test record + recommendation to the LSP.
- §9.4.2–9.4.5 Certification decision is made ONLY by the LSP technical team, in a meeting,
  verified against the assessor's recommendation and evidence, recorded in a Berita Acara,
  and issued as an LSP decision letter (SK LSP). AK.05 remains a recommendation — never
  the certificate decision.

## Certificate lifecycle (§9.4–§9.9) — grounds OQ-10

- Validity: **3 (three) years** (§9.4.6). Issued as a letter and/or card, signed by LSP-appointed
  personnel; handed over only after all certification requirements are met (§9.4.7).
- Recertification (§9.7): apply **min 2 months before expiry**; registration per §9.1,
  assessment per §9.2–9.3, decision per §9.4.
- Surveillance (§9.6): at least **once a year** after issuance (logbook analysis, confirmation
  from direct superior or third party, workplace visit, other methods); results recorded in the
  LSP certificate-holder database.
- Suspension/revocation (§9.5): if the holder breaches holder obligations; directly or after
  warnings.
- Appeal / banding (§9.9): participant may appeal a certification decision; **max 3 days
  after the decision is set** (§9.9.2); LSP provides the appeal form; the appeal team must
  exclude personnel involved in the appealed subject (§9.9.4); appeal decision **max 7
  working days** from receipt of the application (§9.9.6); the appeal decision binds both
  parties (§9.9.7). Applicant rights (§7.1): explanation of the process, right to ask, guarantee
  of confidentiality, right to appeal, certificate if declared competent.

## FR.IA.04B — full structure (from the zip's docx, python-docx)

The form is real and its evidence is the DIT task + presentation — Moza's point 2.
13 tables, 14 paragraphs. Structure:

- Header: FR.IA.04B — DIT – PENILAIAN PROYEK SINGKAT ATAU KEGIATAN TERSTRUKTUR
  LAINNYA; scheme title + number SKM/2037/00013/2/2021/1; TUK Sewaktu/Tempat
  Kerja/Mandiri; Nama Asesor / Nama Asesi / Tanggal.
- PANDUAN BAGI ASESOR (verbatim essence): assess the achievement of the short project
  or other structured activity **through presentation**, per IA.04A DIT. Questions are
  delivered by the assessor **after the assessee presents**; questions may be developed by
  the assessor based on the presentation document and/or the presentation results; the
  questions serve the fulfilment of the **5 competency dimensions**; fill the scope column per
  sector/sub-sector/profession; give the achievement decision from the conclusion of the
  assessee's answers.
- **4 Kelompok Pekerjaan, 13 built-in questions (one per unit):**
  - KP 1 (5 units: 001.1, 005.1, 011.1, 008.1, 009.1) — 5 questions: hazard-identification
    method; hazard factors + measuring instruments + NAB + regulation; K3 at height /
    confined space / heat / electricity + risk control; worker not using mandatory APD +
    prevention; promotive/preventive programmes vs occupational disease (PAK).
  - KP 2 (3 units: 002.1, 006.1, 007.1) — 3 questions: APAR types by media + fire classes;
    why a clear structured P3K system matters + who is responsible; emergency with one
    person unaccounted at the assembly point.
  - KP 3 (3 units: 003.1, 004.1, 010.1) — 3 questions: K3 information not reaching workers;
    work permit / permit to work / SIKA (Pertamina) in the assessee's workplace; why SMK3
    must be integrated with documentation.
  - KP 4 (2 units: 013.1, 012.1) — 2 questions: investigation where the victim did no unsafe
    act and followed procedure; function of the investigation report.
- Each Aspek Penilaian table: scope | Daftar Pertanyaan | Kesesuaian dengan standar
  kompetensi kerja (unit/elemen/KUK) | **Ya | Tidak** per KUK.
- Recommendation row: "Asesi telah memenuhi / belum memenuhi pencapaian seluruh
  kriteria unjuk kerja, direkomendasikan: **Kompeten / Belum Kompeten**" — binary only.
- Signature blocks: Asesi (Nama, Tanda tangan & tanggal) + Asesor (Nama, No. Reg,
  Tanda tangan & tanggal). Assessor signature applies to 04B (already in the v1.2 14-form list).
- Penyusun/validator table: 7 penyusun + 6 validators with MET numbers, dated
  2 November 2024. Names not reproduced here (docs carry no personal names).

Consequence for OQ-02: the LSP expects the **official 04B form with its 13 built-in
